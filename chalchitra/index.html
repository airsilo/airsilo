Why Chalchitra

Your media. Your machine. Full stop.

Most players want your email, your telemetry, your bandwidth and your attention. Chalchitra wants none of it. It opens a file and plays it — beautifully.

wifi_off

Truly offline

No internet connection required — ever. No update pings, no license checks, no analytics, no phoning home. Unplug the cable and it works exactly the same.

shield

Nothing gets uploaded

Your videos are opened directly from your disk and decoded on your machine. There is no server, no cloud, no transcoding queue. Your files never leave your device.

block

No ads. No tracking.

Not a single banner, pop-up, upsell or "recommended" row. Chalchitra has no advertising code and no tracking code inside it at all.

bolt

Hardware accelerated

Decoding is handed to your GPU — AMD, Intel and NVIDIA all supported. Smooth 4K playback without cooking your CPU or draining your battery.

auto_awesome

Modern, minimal UI

No 2005-era menu bars, no confusing settings labyrinth. A clean, dark, distraction-free interface that stays out of the way of your movie.

extension

Built on libmpv

Playback is powered by libmpv-2.dll — the same battle-tested engine behind mpv, famous for playing literally anything you throw at it.

Questions

Good to know

Does Chalchitra really work without internet? expand_more
Yes. Chalchitra makes no network requests at all — no update checks, no license validation, no analytics, no ads. It is designed to run perfectly on an air-gapped machine. Disconnect your Wi-Fi and everything works exactly the same.
Are my videos uploaded anywhere? expand_more
Never. Files are opened straight from your local disk and decoded on your own hardware. There is no cloud, no server and no transcoding queue — your media never leaves your device.
Which formats are supported? expand_more
Chalchitra uses libmpv, so it handles MP4, MKV, MOV, WebM, AVI, WMV, FLV, TS, M2TS and more, with H.264, H.265/HEVC, ProRes, VP9, AV1, MPEG-2 and MPEG-4 video, plus AAC, FLAC, MP3, Opus, AC-3 and DTS audio. HDR10 and 10-bit content are supported, along with SRT, ASS, WebVTT and PGS subtitles.
Do I need to install codecs separately? expand_more
No. Everything needed for playback is bundled with Chalchitra. There are no codec packs, no K-Lite installs and no "media player cannot play this file" errors.
Will it use my GPU? expand_more
Yes. AMD, Intel and NVIDIA hardware acceleration is supported and auto-detected, so 4K and HDR playback stays smooth while your CPU stays cool. If hardware decoding isn't available for a stream, Chalchitra falls back to software decoding automatically.
What about macOS and Linux? expand_more
Both are planned. Version 1.0.0 is Windows 64-bit only — as a setup .exe or a portable .zip. Follow the GitHub repository for release announcements.
Is it free? expand_more
Completely. Chalchitra is free software under the GNU General Public License v3 — no paid tiers, no premium features, no subscription, no ads. You're also free to inspect, modify and redistribute the source code.
