Your files never touch a server

Most “online converters” upload your video to someone else’s computer. VidFlux doesn’t have a server at all — it’s a native desktop app that does every single frame locally.

wifi_off

100% Offline

Works with your Wi-Fi off and your Ethernet unplugged. Install once, convert forever — no license checks, no activation, no phoning home.

cloud_off

Zero Uploads

There is no upload button because there is nothing to upload. Files are read from your disk and written back to your disk.

visibility_off

No Tracking

No analytics, no crash reporting, no telemetry, no accounts. The app makes zero network requests — by design.

speed

Blazing Fast

Hardware encoding offloads the heavy lifting to your GPU, so a job that takes minutes in software can finish in seconds.

memory Built for real hardware

Up to 4K 10-bit HDR — and 8K if your GPU can take it

VidFlux talks directly to your GPU’s media engine, so you keep quality high and render times low.

GPU Accelerated
3 vendors

AMD · Intel · NVIDIA

Automatic detection and support for NVIDIA NVENC, Intel Quick Sync Video and AMD AMF. Pick your encoder or let VidFlux choose the fastest one available.

HDR Ready
10-bit HDR

Up to 4K HDR

Full support for 10-bit color depth and HDR sources. Convert HDR10 footage without flattening it into dull, banded SDR.

Extreme
8K capable

For strong hardware

Got a recent GPU with plenty of VRAM? VidFlux will happily chew through 8K footage at hardware speed.
