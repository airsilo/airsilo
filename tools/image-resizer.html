About CropFlow

CropFlow is an offline image resizer, cropper and compressor that never touches a server. Your image is decoded, edited and re-encoded entirely inside this browser tab.

  • Resize — set an exact pixel width and height, lock the aspect ratio, or pick a quick preset.
  • Crop — drag the box, pull any of the eight handles, or lock a ratio like 1:1 or 16:9.
  • Shapes — rectangular, rounded corners, or a perfect circle cut-out.
  • Compress — switch on “Limit file size” and CropFlow binary-searches JPEG/WebP quality (and image scale, for PNG) until it fits your KB budget.
  • Portals — one-tap presets for passport photos, signatures and thumbnails.

Privacy: no uploads, no analytics, no network calls. Close the tab and everything is gone.
