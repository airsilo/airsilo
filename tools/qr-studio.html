qr_code_2About QRFlux

A complete QR generator that never touches the network.

  • Create — links, plain text, Wi-Fi, email, phone, SMS and contact cards.
  • Style — module shapes, eye shapes, solid or gradient colours, transparent background and a centre logo.
  • Export — crisp PNG (up to 2048 px) or scalable SVG.

Everything — encoding and styling — is computed in this page. No uploads, no accounts, no analytics. Works fully offline once loaded.
